In the Beginning...

Ben and Betty Russom have been happily married for 10 years. They met back on their honeymoon at Arches National Monument with great memories. They have tried to keep the same spice in their marriage as they build on their honeymoon.

This amazing couple makes it a practice to try out new extreme sports together, just for fun and thrill. They said honeymoon combined the wild mountain biking adventure in the incredible dark red rock near Moab, Utah and through Arches National Monument. On this same trip they also spent 3 days white-water rafting down the Colorado River.

Where We are Today...

Extreme Honeymoons has been in business since 1978. Over 2000 couples have enjoyed these high-octane honeymoons. The Russom's have also had seven of their children get married and have enjoyed planning with awesome honeymoons to these lucky couples. One of the benefits of living in Utah is the wide variety of adventure activities in the state.
